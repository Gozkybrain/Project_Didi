# About Project-Didi

**Who is Didi? A digital partner who runs on this machine.**

Didi is voice-first and agent-driven. Today she plays the archivist for a Marvel movie & series archive, and she's built to grow into a dev, data, and sales crew - all local, all orchestrated by her, with you pulling the trigger on anything external.

## Where it lives

There's no rented cloud, no data center in another country. Didi runs on a machine we own, in a building we're in - the app is **live on the internet** at **https://didi.sarcasticgeeks.com**, with the data staying private on our machine.

The whole library and data sits on our own hard drive - a **MacBook Pro M1 Max, 64GB of memory, 4TB of storage**, a real computer doing real work, not a slice of someone's cloud. No company's server is holding our films; our machine is. And because people reach it through a URL instead of an address, no one needs to install anything - they just open the link, from anywhere in the country or the world.

## Didi, the assistant

An AI chat assistant helps you pick what to watch and answers with rich cards, not dry lists. And Didi has a real voice and personality:

- She **hears you** - you talk to the machine directly
- She **speaks back** in a natural African-American female voice
- She mumbles think-sounds while she works on an answer
- You can also **type** instead of speaking - texting feels more natural than a back-and-forth between voices
- Speak, then **correct by text** if anything came out wrong
- You can **switch her voice**, even the gender
- She's **context-aware** - she remembers the conversation, your preferences, and what's happening right now, so her answers fit the moment, not a script
- She answers from the **real data** - the actual records of the collection, never a guess

## The brain (choosing how she thinks)

Didi comes wired to a mix of brains, and you can switch between them any time. Straight from **Ollama**, running right on this machine, she has **Qwen3-32B** for everyday thinking (fast, private, fully offline), **Qwen2.5-Coder** for code and build work, and **Llama 3.2** as a lighter runner. For heavier, non-sensitive lifting she can reach a couple of free models from the **OpenRouter** catalog, like a fallback everyday brain. Her senses are local too - she hears you through a local **whisper** speech model and speaks through her own voice engine. Anything real or sensitive stays on this machine.

## The films, and how she knows them

The archive is the ground truth, and Didi was raised on it. Every title, every character, every first appearance is recorded in our own records on our own disk, and that's the only source she answers from. Ask how many appearances a character has, who meets whom, or what to watch next - she answers from the real files, not from a guess. The collection lives where we live, and so does her memory of it.

One rule runs through the whole crew: **everything internal stays internal, anything that touches the outside world stops at you.**

## Payment (how the movies are paid for)

Watching is pay-to-play. Every signed-in user holds a balance - credits (play money for now) - and unlocks a title by paying from it. Users can transfer credits to each other, and every single payment, transfer, and watch is written into a **transaction log**: who paid, who received, what was watched, when.

This is a rehearsal done properly. Before anything touches real money, we prove the whole flow - balances, transfers, pay-to-watch, a full ledger - with dummy funds. When it's proven, the play money is swapped for a real payment rail.

## What we mean for Nigerian data

We're building toward something simple and ours: **a data center that stays in Nigeria, on hardware we own**.

The idea is simple - our files, our compute, our rules, on a machine that lives here. Right now that's one MacBook (M1 Max, 64GB RAM, 4TB storage), solar-powered, kept on 24/7, reachable through a simple link instead of a public address. It grows from a single app into a small private cloud for a team: real accounts, personal libraries, per-user records and logs. Not "the cloud abroad." A real server in the country whose data it holds.
